## Design Rules

1. **Alternating tones:** Dark (navy) and light (paper) slides for rhythm
2. **Typography:** Large, bold for stats and headlines. Readable body text.
3. **Headshot:** Greg's photo on final slide, bottom-right or integrated
4. **Logo:** RE/MAX logo subtle, usually on final slide
5. **Swipe indicator:** Subtle arrows or dots showing it's a carousel
6. **Mobile-first:** All text readable on phone screens

---

## Content Strategy (Entropy Framework)

When Greg fills in content, it should:

- **L3 (Process):** Demystify the unknown → "Here's what actually happens"
- **L5 (Expertise):** Show insider knowledge → "What most people don't realize..."
- **L6 (Situations):** Address specific fears → "What if X happens? Here's the answer."

**Hook formulas:**
- "Most people think [X]. Here's the truth."
- "The question I get asked most"
- "What actually happens when you [process]"
- "[Number] steps to [outcome]"

**Literary devices:**
- Contrast (myth vs fact, before vs after)
- Specificity (real numbers, real examples)
- Rule of three
- Open loops (create curiosity to swipe)
