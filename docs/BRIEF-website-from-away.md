### Proof: the Saint John story (anonymous — approved)

didn't know a soul on the Island. Six showings in a day, steady follow-up,
all the non-resident information, a list of local tradespeople

easy — the money wasn't: some US banks won't send to Canada, transfers
take time, the lawyer holds a draft before closing. Greg had the lawyers
talking to them early. Both closed. "Give the money more time than the
house."
