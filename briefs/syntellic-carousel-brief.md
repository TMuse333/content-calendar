## Carousel Ideas to Create

### 1. "5 Things Your Website Is Missing" (Educational)
**Format:** MythVsFactCarousel or BuyerObjectionsCarousel style
**Signal:** You need a better website, here's why
**Audience:** Business owners, realtors
**Slides:**
1. Hook: "Your website is costing you clients"
2. No mobile optimization
3. Slow load times
4. No clear CTA
5. Generic template look
6. No SEO foundation
7. CTA: "Let's fix this"

### 2. "Client Transformation: Before vs After" (Social Proof)
**Format:** ThisOrThatCarousel style
**Signal:** We transform basic websites into conversion machines
**Audience:** Business owners considering a rebrand
**Slides:**
1. Hook: "Same business. Different website. 10x more leads."
2. Before screenshot
3. After screenshot
4. Key changes made
5. Results/testimonial
6. CTA

### 3. "How We Got [Client] to Page 1 of Google" (Case Study)
**Format:** ProcessTimelineCarousel style
**Signal:** SEO results are achievable
**Audience:** Business owners frustrated with visibility
**Slides:**
1. Hook: "From invisible to #1 on Google"
2. Starting point (where they ranked)
3. Step 1: Technical SEO fixes
4. Step 2: Content optimization
5. Step 3: Local SEO
6. Results screenshot
7. CTA
